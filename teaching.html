Teaching

Stevens Institute of Technology

I served as a Course Instructor at Stevens Institute of Technology, where I taught three master’s-level core courses, each worth three credits.

BIA-660-B: Web Mining and Natural Language Processing

Spring 2024

This course examines web mining and text analysis techniques using Python. Topics include:

  • Collecting data from the web
  • Parsing and cleaning structured and unstructured text
  • Foundations of machine learning and deep learning
  • Basics of natural language processing
  • Text mining with large language models

BIA-580-C: Mathematical Foundations of Business Analytics

Fall 2023

This course covers the mathematical and statistical concepts underlying business analytics. Business applications connect theory with practice, while examples and assignments develop Python programming skills. (Some lecture materials can be found below.)
